## Welcome to xRenovation!

### xRenovation is a place where you can share and discuss houses built under the renovation program

#### What can i do within the app?

- Add Houses with their specs and your thoughts on them

- Add comments with your thoughts on specific house

- Add correction if you think that the house specs are wrong or might be extended

- Simply admire the beautiful houses !

#### Technologies:

- Kotlin, Flow, Coroutines + MVVM

- Jetpack Navigation

- Dagger

- Retrofit2

- Glide

- FireBase Auth, FireBase Storage, FireBase Database

- Yandex MapKit

- Yandex Cloud
